2. Enter the Treatment ID from Step 4

4. Enter quantity

#### Step 6: View Complete Records
1. View the patient's complete treatment history
2. Check consultation reports
3. Verify medicine prescription records

**Expected Result**: Complete workflow executes successfully with all auto-generated IDs working correctly
